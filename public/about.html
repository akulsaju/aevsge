What is this, in plain English?

A shared money notebook that keeps working when the internet is bad, down, or being attacked.

The problem

Imagine a village after a storm. No internet, no bank. Five people each keep a notebook of who owns what (alice has 500, bob has 100). They can only shout notes to each other, and most shouts (80%) get lost in the wind. How do you make sure everyone's notebook ends up the same, and nobody can cheat?

What you see on the demo page

The 5 circles
Five devices (phones, radios). Each has its own notebook. The two numbers inside are the balances for alice / bob that this device believes.
The moving dots
Messages. Blue = delivered. Red = lost on the way. Devices keep re-sending, so eventually enough get through.
"Do all devices agree?"
Green ✔ means every notebook matches. Red numbers show which device is behind.

Fake-node attack (Sybil)

A "Sybil" attack is one cheater pretending to be many people. The button Attack with fake node floods the mesh with a lie ("alice has a billion!"). With Block unknown nodes on, devices ignore anyone not on their list. Untick it and watch the lie spread.

The honest limits

  • Double-spend: if alice spends the same 400 on both sides of a split, the notebooks will agree afterward but only keep one of the two spends. They agree, but the money isn't conserved. Real systems need extra rules (consensus or real ZK balance proofs). The Story: spend the same money twice button shows this.
  • The "proof" is a stand-in, not real cryptography. The trusted list is fixed, not real identity.
  • Everything runs in your browser; the optional Ask server button calls a small Vercel function.

Quick 60-second tour

  1. Open the demo. Leave "Messages lost" at 80%, press Send. Watch the red/blue dots, then the green ✔.
  2. Press Try to cheat. See the rejection in "What just happened".
  3. Turn Split network on, send on two different devices, turn it off. They reconcile.
  4. Press Attack with fake node. It's blocked. Untick the block box and try again.
  5. Press Run automated audit to see 4/4 requirements pass.
